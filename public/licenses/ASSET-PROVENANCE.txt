QUIET HARBOR DEVELOPMENT ASSET PROVENANCE

The raster illustrations and synthesized audio in public/assets were authored
procedurally for this repository using scripts/generate-assets.py. They contain
no stock art, sampled recordings, emoji, SVG assets, third-party melodies, or
external image-generation output. No external visual or audio source license
is required. Distribution rights for these original project assets follow the
repository owner's project licensing; this notice does not assign a new public
license to the project.

These are development assets, NOT human-approved production artwork/audio.
The specification's image-generation and human review gates remain pending.
See docs/ASSETS.md for the delivered scope, limitations and reproduction steps.
